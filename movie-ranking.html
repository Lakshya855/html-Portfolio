The Best Movies of all time according to Raghav

My Top 3 MOVIES of all time

1. BARFI

This will always rank first for me because this film shows how versatile acting can be. "a movie in which the leads never said anything"

2. HARRY POTTER SERIES

For all the children and even the adults this will tell how interesting one's life can be when they discover more about themselves and not about others."A spell that created history"

3. YEH JAWANI HAI DEEWANI

For all the youngsters this will tell how important it is to live your life to the fullest and not to worry about what others think of you."A movie that will make you want to travel the world"
